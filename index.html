What it does

Before a revision goes for approval, a Steel review argues against it. Every claim and clause is challenged, every reference is checked, and each point is marked: stands, weakened or falls. The review is filed with the revision, and it learns from what your approvers accept and reject, inside your workspace only. A named person still approves.

What we get asked first.

What is Smart Docs Ai?

Document control with AI inside. Controlled documents, numbered revisions, approvals, an audit trail, a revision log and a PDF of every edit, with lessons learned kept beside the documents they changed.

Is it a document management system?

No. Storage tools keep files. Smart Docs Ai keeps the record: which revision is in force, who approved it, what changed and why. You can keep using your storage underneath it.

Who is Doxie?

Our visual agent, the animated face of Smart Docs Ai. She shows what the product does: every folder filed, every revision kept on record. She is a drawing, not a person, and she approves nothing. A named person approves every revision.

What is a Steel review?

An adversarial check before approval. It takes the other side of the revision on purpose, checks every reference it cites, and writes a verdict for each point that is kept with the revision record. It learns from your approvers' decisions inside your workspace, and nothing you store trains a model for anyone else.

Does the AI change my documents?

It drafts, summarises and suggests, and every suggestion is labelled. A named person approves every revision. The AI never writes to a released revision.

Where are my files kept?

Where you choose. Hosted by us, or on your own storage with the installable version. Either way they are yours, you can export everything at any time, and nothing you store trains a model for anyone else.

Does it meet ISO 9001?

It is built around clause 7.5, documented information: creation, approval, revision, distribution and retention, with the records an auditor asks for. We will say plainly which standards it meets and which it does not yet.
